# ShortList revenue rhythm

**Status:** deferred MVP 3+ planning input — not authority for outreach,
pricing, customer contact, paid claims, or automation.
**Owner:** Chris / SuccessByCS

This document describes a deliberately manual, one-person operating rhythm for
testing whether ShortList can become a useful paid service after MVP 1 is live,
truthful, and producing a credible free assessment. It is not part of MVP 1 or
MVP 2 implementation.

## The first-revenue path

The shortest intended learning path is:

1. a credible landing page and working domain-entry journey;
2. a useful free Minimum Assessment;
3. one strong, clearly labelled worked example;
4. personalised outreach to one narrow local-business category; then
5. a paid detailed report with a clear next step.

ShortList helps a small local service business understand whether it is
discoverable, understandable, and sufficiently evidenced to be citable or
recommendable in an AI-assisted answer. It must never promise a first-place
rank, a permanent result, leads, or revenue.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Manual activity | Intended output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Choose one category or location; research 10–20 suitable businesses; record observable visibility gaps and a respectful outreach angle. | Small, sourced target list. |
| Tuesday | Send personalised outreach; improve one acquisition asset or worked example using real observations. | Relevant first messages and one improved asset. |
| Wednesday | Follow up; review free assessments; speak with interested prospects. | Replies, learning, and qualified conversations. |
| Thursday | Hold sales calls; deliver paid work only after its scope is approved; prepare proposals; review product feedback. | Clear next actions and customer evidence. |
| Friday | Clean the pipeline; score results; refine messaging; choose the next weekly experiment. | Updated scorecard and an explicit next hypothesis. |

## Weekly scorecard

Record counts and concise qualitative notes. A number alone is not evidence of
product value.

| Stage | Measure | What to note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Suitable accounts researched | Category, location, source links, observed gap. |
| Outreach | Personalised messages sent | Channel, consent basis, variation used. |
| Response | Replies and positive replies | Objections, useful language, disqualifiers. |
| Product | Domains assessed and free reports delivered | Failure/limited-evidence outcomes separately. |
| Sales | Paid-report conversations, proposals, sales, revenue | No pricing or revenue claim until actually observed. |
| Learning | Repeated objections, category patterns, product feedback | Decision to retain, change, or stop the experiment. |

## Marketing asset to sales-journey connection

| Asset | Sales-journey purpose | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain-language landing page | Explains the free domain assessment and invites a low-friction first step. | No ranking, lead, or revenue guarantee. |
| Worked example | Makes evidence, limitations, and recommended actions concrete. | Use real consented evidence or label it illustrative. |
| Educational content | Helps a business owner understand visibility, structured data, reviews, and corroborating sources. | Explain technical terms; do not use unexplained GEO/AEO jargon. |
| Personalised observation | Gives an outreach recipient a specific, useful reason to try the free assessment. | Use public evidence only; do not make unverified ranking claims. |
| Paid report / implementation offer | Provides a clear next step after useful free value. | Pricing, scope, and terms require a later explicit decision. |

## Four-week experiment rules

Run one narrow category/location experiment for four weeks before widening.
Start with 10–20 carefully selected businesses per week, using only approved
channels and consent-aware handling.

- **Persist** when qualified replies, assessment use, and conversations show a
  repeatable evidence-led problem worth solving.
- **Change the vertical** when the category has little observable need, weak
  willingness to engage, or unsuitable buying conditions.
- **Adjust the free assessment** when prospects understand the observation but
  do not find the free result useful enough to act on.
- **Revise messaging** when recipients do not understand the problem, confuse
  ShortList with a ranking guarantee, or repeatedly ask the same question.
- **Test pricing** only after the paid offer, delivery boundary, and pricing
  hypothesis have explicit approval and an evidence-led buyer conversation
  supports the test.

## Required future decisions

Before this rhythm is operated, the Marketing Foundation and Sales & Customer
Acquisition Engine milestones must record the target vertical, paid offer,
pricing hypothesis, permitted outreach channel, sender identity, consent and
suppression rules, exclusions, privacy handling, and success criteria. No
automation, unsolicited bulk contact, or medical/legal/financial advice is
authorised by this document.
